Frequently asked questions

Where is Lumen after I close its window?
Lumen lives in the menu bar (sparkles icon). If the icon is hidden behind the notch, open Lumen again from Launchpad or Finder and the window comes back.

How do I restore Lumen Pro on another Mac?
Open Lumen, click “Learn More” or any Pro wallpaper, then choose “Restore Purchases”. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Will it drain my battery?
Wallpapers draw at 30 frames per second by default and pause automatically when your desktop is covered, when the display sleeps or in Low Power Mode. For the longest battery life, choose Battery Saver quality in Settings.

Can I use my own photos or videos?
Yes, with Lumen Pro: click Add ▸ Add Image… or Add Video… in the Lumen window.
