Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Tap "Continue without an account" to study straight away. Signing in is optional and saves your profile.

The sign-in link didn't work.
Open the email on the same phone where Metirion is installed and tap the button. Each link works once and expires after a short time, so request a new one from the sign-in screen if needed. Check your spam folder if the email hasn't arrived.

Which papers are covered?
ACCA Applied Knowledge: Business and Technology (BT), Management Accounting (MA) and Financial Accounting (FA), with chapter tests and timed mock exams.

How do I delete my account?
In the app go to Settings → Account → Delete account, or see how to delete your account.
