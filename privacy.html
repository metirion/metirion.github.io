Summary

Metirion is a study app for ACCA Applied Knowledge exams (BT, MA and FA). You can use it without an account, and then your study progress stays on your own device. If you choose to sign in, we keep a small profile and back up your study progress (test scores, mistakes and topic progress) so we can support you and improve the app. Metirion and our partner Klarion can see this information. We do not show ads, we do not sell your data, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who we are

Metirion ("Metirion", "we", "us") develops and operates the Metirion app and this website. Contact: metirionapp@gmail.com.

Metirion is independent study material. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by ACCA.

Information we collect

If you use the app without an account, your study progress and settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive them.

If you sign in (with a one-time link sent to your email), we collect:

InformationWhy
Email addressTo send your sign-in link and identify your account
NameTo personalise the app
Phone number (optional)Only if you choose to give it, as an extra contact detail on your profile
Account IDA random identifier created for your account
Study profile: student status, study mode, subjects, exam session, occupation, college, university or coaching centre, how you heard about MetirionTo tailor the app to your exams and study plan
Study activity: chapter test and mock exam results (paper, chapter, score, date), questions you answered incorrectly, topic progress (Not studied, Learning, Reviewed, Mastered), study streaks and achievements. We also record how you use study content: which topics you open (once per day), flashcard sessions, confidence ratings you set, and whether you start, finish or leave a test or mock exam part-way (with questions answered and time spent)To save your progress, show you how you are doing, and help us and our partner support students and improve the questions

Study activity is uploaded only while you are signed in. Activity from before you signed in that is already on your device is uploaded the first time you sign in on this version of the app or later.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, payment details, health information, browsing history, or advertising identifiers. The app contains no analytics or advertising tools.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account working.
  • To personalise your study experience.
  • To reply to you if you contact support.
  • To understand overall usage, such as which topics students find hardest, so we can improve the app and its questions.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Who can see your progress

Signed-in students' names, contact details, study profile and study progress can be viewed by:

  • Metirion, through a private, sign-in-protected dashboard.
  • Klarion, our partner that helps promote Metirion. A small number of named Klarion team members can view students' names, contact details (email address and phone number, if given), study profile and study progress through the same dashboard, with read-only access, to support students and review how the partnership is going. Klarion may not sell or share this information, or use it to market to you without your separate consent.

Other students cannot see your information. If you do not want this, you can use Metirion without an account, or delete your account at any time.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the app. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase – secure database and sign-in service that stores account and profile data.
  • Google (Gmail) – sends the sign-in emails.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play – distribute the app. Their own privacy policies apply to your use of their stores.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) – the app may link to the Metirion WhatsApp channel. If you choose to follow it, WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies. Followers of a channel cannot see each other's phone numbers.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Security and storage

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access is restricted by account: each student can only reach their own records, and the Metirion and Klarion team members described above have separate read-only access. We keep your account data, including your study activity, until you delete your account.

Delete your account and data

In the app: open Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and profile data from our servers.

By email: if you can't use the app, email metirionapp@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and ask us to delete your account. We'll delete it and confirm, normally within 30 days.

Deleting your account also deletes your uploaded study activity. Study progress kept only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete the information we hold about you by emailing metirionapp@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA or India's DPDP Act), you may also have the right to object to processing or complain to your local data protection authority.

Age

Metirion is intended for users aged 16 and over. It is not designed for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be shown in the app.
