Privacy Policy

Lumen Live Wallpapers for macOS · Last updated: November 4, 2026

Lumen does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your Mac

Your wallpapers, settings and any photos or videos you add are stored only on your Mac, inside the app's own private folder. They are never uploaded anywhere.

Purchases

Lumen Pro is purchased through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; the developer never receives your payment details or Apple Account information.

Websites you choose

If you set a website as your wallpaper, that website is loaded directly from its own server and is subject to that website's own privacy policy.

Children

Lumen does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
