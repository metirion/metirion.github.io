Metirion Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

Metirion is a study app for students preparing for ACCA exams. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Contact: metirionapp@gmail.com.

1. You can use Metirion without an account

You can use all of Metirion without signing in. Without an account, your study progress (test results, flashcards, mistakes, streaks, settings) is stored only on your own device, and we do not receive it.

2. What we collect if you create an account

If you choose to sign in, we collect:

  • Email address – used to send you a sign-in link and to identify your account.
  • Name – shown inside the app.
  • Phone number (optional).
  • About your studies – whether you are new to ACCA or already studying, how you study (self-study or coaching center) and the coaching center name, whether you are a student or working professional, your college or university (optional), the subjects you are interested in, and your planned exam session.
  • How you heard about Metirion (optional).

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

3. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To personalise the app and understand, in aggregate, who uses Metirion so we can improve it.
  • To contact you about your account or to respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not show advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. The app does not use third-party analytics or advertising tools.

4. Who processes your data

  • Supabase – stores account and profile data and handles sign-in. Servers are located in India (Mumbai).
  • Google (Gmail) – delivers the sign-in emails we send you.

These providers process data only to provide those services to us.

5. Deleting your account and data

In the app: open Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm. This permanently deletes your account and all the information listed in section 2 from our systems.

By email: if you cannot access the app, email metirionapp@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and ask us to delete your account. We will complete the request within 30 days.

Study progress stored on your device is not sent to us; you can clear it in the app (Settings → Reset my progress) or by uninstalling the app.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your account information until you delete your account.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing metirionapp@gmail.com.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and each account can access only its own records. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

9. Children

Metirion is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Independence from ACCA

Metirion is independent study material. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by ACCA.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
